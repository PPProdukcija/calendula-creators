What Calendula does

One label, read for your whole family

Photograph the ingredients or scan a barcode. Calendula shows at once who it fits, who should take a look and who should avoid it, based on the notes you keep for each person.

Every ingredient, gently explained

Tap any word on the label: what E129 is, what an emulsifier does, where added sugar hides. Plain words, with the EU and US rules behind them.

Notes for up to 6 people

Allergies, pregnancy, breastfeeding, baby, toddler, intolerances and preferences. These notes stay on the phone and are never sent anywhere.

Skincare too

Shampoo, lotion, deodorant, perfume: the same plain-language reading, with fragrance allergens and EU warnings called out.

Compare two products side by side

A composition score with its method and sources always visible. No brand partnerships and no ads, so there is nothing to sell you.

How it works

  1. I send you a code for a free year of Calendula Premium. Try the app whenever it suits you, in the store and at home. You don't have to post anything.
  2. If you like it, you get your personal code and a personal link that already includes it. The link opens the App Store with your discount applied.
  3. You put the link in your bio or pinned comment and mention your code in videos. People who already installed the app can type the code under "Redeem a code" in Settings.
  4. Apple counts every purchase made with your code. You earn on each one, and I send you Apple's numbers every month.

How and when you get paid.

  • Your very first sale is paid right away, as soon as it shows up in App Store Connect.
  • After that, each month is paid by the end of the second month after it. Example: November sales are paid by January 31. That's when Apple has paid me for that month.
  • Payment goes through Payoneer, to your Payoneer account or straight to your bank account. Minimum payout is $20; smaller amounts roll over to the next month.
  • Every month you get a statement: a screenshot of your code's purchases from App Store Connect, your commission, any refund deduction and the amount sent.

Honest limit: for now you see the numbers through my monthly statement, not in your own dashboard. They are Apple's numbers, unchanged.

The fair-play rules

  1. Codes work only for people who have never subscribed to Calendula, so every purchase you earn on is a real new customer.
  2. Refunds: each month's commission is reduced by that month's refund rate, as shown in my App Store financial report. Refunds that arrive later come off the next payout.
  3. Label your posts as sponsored: use the platform's paid-partnership setting and add #ad. The free year counts as a connection too.
  4. No medical claims. Calendula explains what a label says; never say a product is safe for pregnancy, a baby or an allergy because of the app.
  5. Show what's on the label, without verdicts on a specific brand ("toxic", "the worst").
  6. Your audience should be adults. Children can appear on camera only with a parent's consent. I review your first video before it goes up.
  7. Please don't post your codes on coupon or deal sites, and never offer anything in exchange for App Store ratings or reviews.
  8. Either of us can stop with 14 days' notice. Anything you've already earned is still paid.
